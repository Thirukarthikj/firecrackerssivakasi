Three Decades of Excellence

Preserving Sivakasi's Legacy with Modern Safety Science

Established in 1994 in Konampatti near Sattur Road, Sivakasi Trading Agency began with a simple mission: bringing authentic, joyful, and impeccably safe Sivakasi pyrotechnics directly from master artisans to celebrations across India.

Today, our 40-acre licensed manufacturing enclave features temperature-controlled raw material chambers, automated powder batch mixers, and precision fuse-timing assembly bays that meet stringent PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) standards.
