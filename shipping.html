Surface Road Transportation Protocol

In strict accordance with Indian explosive safety statutory laws, fireworks transport is handled exclusively via authorized surface road logistics. Air freight is strictly prohibited. Orders should be booked well in advance of peak festive periods to avoid transit congestion.
